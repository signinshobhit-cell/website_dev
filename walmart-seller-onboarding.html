OFFICIAL INDIA REQUIREMENTS

What should you prepare before applying?

Walmart’s India seller guidance lists the following. Requirements vary with business type and may change; use the current portal checklist when applying.

  • Business-name and address verification documents.
  • A history of marketplace or e-commerce success.
  • A US address for customer returns.
  • An eligible payout provider, such as Payoneer or PingPong.
  • GSTIN and incorporation documents, as applicable to your entity.

Walmart’s India guidance explains a document-based verification route for sellers without a US tax ID. Do not assume a US company is mandatory for every Indian applicant.

Walmart onboarding questions.

Can Yuvglobal guarantee seller approval?

No. Walmart reviews the application and makes the approval decision. We assist with readiness, information preparation and agreed onboarding tasks.

Do I need an Indian GSTIN or a US tax ID?

Walmart’s India guidance lists GSTIN and entity-specific documentation and describes verification for Indian sellers without a US tax ID. Review the current requirements for your business type.

How long does Walmart onboarding take?

It depends on document completeness, verification requests, catalogue work and Walmart’s review. We agree a work plan after assessing your starting point; a fixed approval date is not promised.

Can you support an existing Walmart account?

Yes, you can request a defined scope for catalogue preparation, connector coordination, fulfilment planning or ongoing operational support. Share the issue first so we can assess the work.

Is Yuvglobal an official Walmart partner?

We provide independent consultancy and do not claim official Walmart partner status. Our professional service fee is separate from any charges levied by Walmart or other providers.
