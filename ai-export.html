USE AI WHERE IT HELPS

Six practical ways to support your export desk.

These are suggested workflows—not automated approvals. Each combines an AI-assisted task with a specific review step.

01 · MARKET RESEARCH

Compare potential markets

Organise sourced trade data, demand indicators and channel information into a comparison.

Check: Source dates, HS-code coverage and destination requirements using Trade Connect and official publications.

02 · BUYER RESEARCH

Prepare a buyer-check brief

Summarise a company’s public website, registry records and supplied documents. List inconsistencies and questions.

Check: Identity, registration and applicable screening results independently. A plausible AI summary is not a credit assessment.

03 · COMMUNICATION

Draft clearer enquiries & quotes

Turn approved specifications, quantities and commercial terms into an email or follow-up draft.

Check: Price, currency, Incoterm, lead time, certifications and payment terms before sending.

04 · DOCUMENTATION

Spot mismatched information

Compare approved invoice and packing-list data and identify missing fields or inconsistent quantities.

Check: Original records, totals and mandatory fields. Your export team or customs broker approves filing documents.

05 · LC PREPARATION

Make an LC review checklist

Organise document requirements, shipment dates and presentation conditions from a redacted LC draft.

Check: Every clause with your bank or trade-finance adviser. AI cannot confirm bank acceptance or legal enforceability.

06 · DAILY OPERATIONS

Create repeatable team workflows

Build reusable task templates, handover notes and exception checklists for enquiry-to-shipment work.

Check: Assign an owner, define approval points and measure the results using real examples.
