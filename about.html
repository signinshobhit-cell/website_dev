About Flexlyf Commerce

A decade of expertise. Thousands of MSMEs transformed. One clear mission.

OUR STORY

Built on IIFT Excellence

Flexlyf Commerce Private Limited was founded with a singular vision: to empower Indian MSMEs with the knowledge, certifications, and support they need to compete confidently in global markets.

Our founder, Shobhit Verma, an alumnus of the prestigious Indian Institute of Foreign Trade (IIFT), New Delhi, recognized that countless Indian businesses had world-class products but lacked the regulatory expertise to navigate international trade complexities.

Today, we've grown into a comprehensive trade consultancy serving 500+ MSMEs across India, helping them achieve certifications, secure licenses, conduct product testing, arrange inspections, and obtain trade insurance — all under one roof.

OUR MISSION

Empowering Indian MSMEs to Go Global

We believe that every Indian manufacturer and exporter, regardless of size, deserves access to world-class trade expertise. Our mission is to democratize international trade knowledge and make global expansion accessible, affordable, and achievable for MSMEs across India.

Through comprehensive support spanning certification, licensing, testing, inspection, and insurance, we remove barriers and build confidence for businesses ready to compete on the global stage.

OUR VISION

Becoming India's Most Trusted Trade Partner

We envision a future where Indian MSMEs are recognized globally for quality, compliance, and reliability. Flexlyf Commerce aims to be the catalyst for this transformation — the trusted advisor that guides businesses through every step of their international journey.

By 2030, we aim to support 5,000+ Indian businesses in establishing successful global operations, contributing to India's position as a leading export nation.
