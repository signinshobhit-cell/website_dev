APEDA RCMC registration assistance

Exporting agricultural or processed food products? Start by checking whether your products fall within APEDA’s scope. Yuvglobal assists with APEDA registration-cum-membership certificate preparation and the DGFT e-RCMC route, rather than treating every food export as the same application.

Check the right registration for your product

Share your product description, HS code if known, and target destination. We review the relevant council or commodity board before recommending APEDA work. Registration is one part of export readiness; it does not replace product, food-safety, customs or destination requirements.

APEDA registration and the official application route

APEDA’s official guidance directs RCMC issuance through the DGFT portal. We help organise the application information and supporting records, check consistency with your exporter profile and coordinate responses to document queries.

Information for an APEDA consultation

Bring your IEC details, business constitution, authorised contact, product list and existing registration information. Tell us whether you are a manufacturer, merchant exporter or another business type. We confirm the current checklist and applicable scope before requesting documents through an agreed private channel.

Keep RCMC and transaction-specific certificates separate

If you need an APEDA RCAC or another product-specific document, describe the transaction separately. An RCMC enquiry should not be treated as approval for every consignment. We review current requirements and explain the additional work before proceeding.

Application support and transparent quotation

We provide a scope-based quote for document review, preparation and coordination. Official authority charges, where applicable, are distinct from our service charges. APEDA and the relevant authorities decide issuance and eligibility; we do not guarantee approval or a fixed completion date.

Support in Delhi, Uttar Pradesh and Mumbai

Yuvglobal’s corporate office is in New Delhi and registered office is in Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh. We support businesses in Mumbai and across India through remote consultation and document coordination. Collection, on-site visits and service availability are confirmed for your location; Mumbai is a service area, not a listed Yuvglobal office.
