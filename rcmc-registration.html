START WITH THE RIGHT APPLICATION

From council selection to registration.

New exporters

We map your principal products/services and check whether RCMC, membership or a commodity-specific registration applies. Assistance covers new applications, renewals, amendments and authority queries.

New importers

Importing alone does not automatically require an export council RCMC. If you plan to export too, or need a specific authorisation or board licence, we assess the relevant requirement before recommending registration.

1. Share your trade profile

Products/services, HS codes, business activity, IEC status and target markets. We provide a council-specific document checklist.

2. Confirm the council & fees

We check jurisdiction, eligibility and the application route. Council charges and our professional fee are explained separately.

3. Prepare & follow up

Application preparation and submission assistance, responses to queries and registration-record handover. The authority decides approval.

Check eligibility before paying for registration

Under DGFT Notification 36/2026–27, the FTP RCMC/Certificate of Registration requirement is exempted for export consignments with FOB value not exceeding ₹3 lakh. This does not waive every sector-specific approval or guarantee eligibility for a scheme. We check your shipment and intended benefit before advising.
