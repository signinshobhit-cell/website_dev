CLEAR TERMS. BETTER PREPARATION.

Understand the facility. Prepare the right records.

LC: a document-based payment undertaking

A letter of credit sets the documents and conditions for payment by the issuing bank. We help you review the checklist and coordinate the paperwork, so shipment and payment terms fit together.

BG: support for a contractual obligation

A bank guarantee supports an obligation such as contract performance or an advance payment. We help organise the request and review wording with your bank.

Flexlyf provides advisory, documentation and coordination support. Banks and regulated institutions issue facilities and decide limits, pricing, security and approval.
