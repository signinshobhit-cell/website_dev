Most Popular

Pre-Shipment Inspection

Final random inspection when goods are 100% produced and at least 80% packed for export.

  • Quantity Verification
  • Random Sampling (AQL Standards)
  • Visual Quality Check
  • Packaging & Marking Review
  • On-site Function Testing

During Production Inspection

Conducted when 15-80% of production is complete to catch issues early.

  • Production Line Audit
  • Semi-finished Goods Check
  • Process Compliance
  • Early Defect Detection
✅

Initial Production Check

Inspection after first production run to verify setup and initial output quality.

  • First Article Inspection
  • Raw Material Verification
  • Production Setup Review
  • Sample Approval

Container Loading Supervision

  • Container Condition Check
  • Loading Process Monitoring
  • Quantity Confirmation
  • Seal Verification

  • Production Capability Review
