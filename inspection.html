Quality Inspection & Audit Services

Verify product quality and factory compliance before shipment. Reduce returns, protect your brand, and ensure buyer satisfaction.

Why Independent Inspection Matters

Shipping defective goods can be catastrophic for an exporter's reputation and finances. Returns, chargebacks, and lost future orders are real risks when quality control is overlooked.

Flexlyf Commerce provides independent, third-party inspection services across India. Our qualified inspectors verify quantity, workmanship, packaging, and function against your specific requirements or international standards (AQL). We give you the confidence to ship only what meets expectations.

Inspection Workflow

From request to report in 4 simple steps.

1

Request & Brief

Submit your requirements, product details, and checklist (or use our standard).

2

Schedule & Pay

We coordinate with the factory for a suitable date and confirm the booking.

3

On-Site Inspection

Our inspector visits the factory, performs checks, and documents findings with photos.

4

Digital Report

You receive a detailed PDF report within 24 hours with Pass/Fail recommendation.
