Why Cargo Insurance is Essential

Secure Your International Trade Investments

International shipping involves multiple risks - from rough seas and accidents to theft and customs delays. At Flexlyf Commerce, we partner with leading insurers to provide comprehensive cargo insurance that protects your goods throughout the entire logistics chain.

Whether you're exporting textiles to Europe or importing machinery from China, our insurance solutions ensure you're covered against unforeseen losses, giving you peace of mind to focus on growing your business.

⚠️

Did You Know?

Without cargo insurance, carriers' liability is limited to approximately ₹20 per kg under international conventions. A single incident could result in catastrophic financial loss for your business.
