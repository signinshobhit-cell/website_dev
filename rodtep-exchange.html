Before you sell

Questions about selling RoDTEP & RoSCTL scrips.

How is my payout calculated?

Single scrip face value × buying rate ÷ 100. The schedule is 75% below ₹1,000; 85% from ₹1,000 to below ₹10,000; 90% from ₹10,000 to below ₹1 lakh; 95% from ₹1 lakh to below ₹5 lakh; and 99% from ₹5 lakh through ₹50 lakh. Larger scrips need a manual quote. Both schemes use this schedule.

Can I combine several scrips for a higher rate?

No. The rate slab applies to the face value of each individual scrip. Calculate each scrip separately, then contact us if you have several to sell.

Is the displayed payout final?

It is an indicative estimate based on the entered face value. The final amount, any applicable deductions and settlement terms are confirmed in writing after verification. The calculator does not reserve a rate, verify a scrip or complete a sale.

Why do you ask for the port code?

It helps our desk identify and review your scrip details. The current buying-rate schedule is the same across ports; entering a code does not verify that port or your scrip.

Can an exporter or a CHA send a sale enquiry?

Yes. Exporters can enquire about their own scrips, and CHAs or freight forwarders can coordinate for clients. The scrip holder’s identity and authority to sell must be confirmed before a transaction.

What if my scrip is close to expiry or partly used?

Share its number, available balance and validity with the desk before agreeing a sale. We need to review those details; entering the original face value alone does not verify the balance available for transfer.

Do I need to upload KYC documents for an estimate?

No. The calculator asks only for the scheme, single scrip face value and port code. Contact our desk after checking the estimate to confirm how to share the required documents. No KYC documents are uploaded by this page.

When will I receive payment?

Our desk will confirm the payment timeline and transfer sequence with you before the transaction. Do not initiate a transfer until the buyer details and settlement terms are agreed.
