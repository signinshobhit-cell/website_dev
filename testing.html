Accredited Product Testing Services

Validate quality, safety, and compliance with global standards. From raw materials to finished goods — we test it all.

Why Product Testing is Critical for Exports

International buyers demand proof of quality and safety. Whether it's food safety parameters for the EU, heavy metal limits for US toys, or fabric composition for global apparel brands, non-compliance can lead to costly recalls, rejected shipments, and brand damage.

Flexlyf Commerce partners with NABL and ISO accredited laboratories to provide precise, reliable testing reports accepted worldwide. We help you interpret standards, select the right tests, and ensure your products pass scrutiny at customs and buyer docks.
