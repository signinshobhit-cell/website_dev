Accredited Product Testing Services

Validate material quality, food safety, and custom parameters. Eliminate cargo rejection risks with worldwide accepted reports.

Why Product Testing is Critical for Exporters

International customs and B2B buyers enforce precise quality metrics. Whether it is strict pesticide thresholds for EU food imports, chemical substance checks for retail goods, or exact fabric construction details for apparel distribution, non-compliance immediately results in container detention, costly terminal rejections, and direct profit margin penalties.

Flexlyf Commerce manages testing paths via state-of-the-art **NABL accredited and ISO/IEC 17025 certified** laboratories. We evaluate international standards dynamically, isolate the correct testing matrices for your batches, and deliver clear, verified Certificates of Analysis (CoA) to clear port desks smoothly.

Food & Agriculture

Comprehensive trace screening, moisture profiling, and biological assays for fresh horticulture and value-dense items.

  • Pesticide residue testing limits (MRLs)
  • Aflatoxin B1/G1 & Mycotoxin evaluation
  • Moisture & ash metrics for **dehydrated mango slices**
  • Particle screening for **organic hibiscus herbal powder**

Textiles & Apparel

Verification workflows mapping tensile resilience, yarn density, and international banned substance guidelines.

  • Fiber composition blends (Cotton vs Poly)
  • Colorfastness matching, crocking & shrinkage rates
  • Banned Azo Dyes & Free Formaldehyde isolation
  • Flammability parameter codes verification

Electronics & Electrical

Heavy metal screening and restriction processing verifying components meet international manufacturing mandates.

  • RoHS Directive validation (Lead, Cadmium, Mercury)
  • REACH SVHC high-concern chemical profiling
  • Electrical safety standards (IEC parameters)
  • Lithium Battery shipping tests (UN38.3 compliance)

Pharma & Nutraceuticals

Stringent purity analysis and microbiological counts for bulk active materials, supplements, and cosmetics.

  • Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient (API) assay checks
  • Dissolution, breaking threshold & particle sizing
  • Total Aerobic Microbial Count (TAMC) profiles
  • Heavy metals tracing inside cosmetic color bases

Chemicals & Polymers

Composition checking and structural material logs for raw formulations and industrial resins.

  • Purity thresholds and detailed material parsing
  • Viscosity grading, density and flash point benchmarks
  • Tensile modulus and elongation break parameters
  • Differential Scanning Calorimetry (DSC) thermal runs

Packaging & Materials

Structural integrity testing and toxic transit risk audits specifically built for shipping boxes and materials.

  • Drop, compression, and burst factor calibrations
  • Water Vapor Transmission Rate (WVTR) metrics
  • Global Migration Testing (Food contact safety logs)
  • Heavy metals in packaging components verification
