UNDERSTAND THE OPPORTUNITY

What is e-commerce export?

Selling goods from India to overseas customers through an online marketplace or your own website. Orders can ship directly from India, or you can send inventory to an overseas warehouse for local fulfilment. The right route depends on your product, demand, delivery promise and total cost.

Direct-to-customer parcels

Dispatch orders from India through a suitable registered courier or postal channel. Plan delivery charges, export documents, destination duties and returns for each order.

Overseas inventory

Ship stock ahead of demand to a warehouse or fulfilment provider. Plan destination import obligations, storage fees, inventory visibility and unsold-stock handling.

YOUR FIRST EXPORT

A clear path to your first overseas order.

  1. Choose the market

    Check demand, channel fit, competition and the product’s destination requirements.

  2. Prepare the business

    Confirm IEC and applicable registrations. Prepare product, bank and seller-account information.

  3. Price the whole journey

    Include platform fees, packaging, freight, duties, taxes, currency conversion and possible returns.

  4. Ship & reconcile

    Prepare documents, coordinate dispatch and tracking, then match sales, shipment and payment records.

Need working capital?

The March 2026 e-commerce credit guidelines provide eligible MSMEs with credit limits up to ₹50 lakh for direct exports or ₹5 crore for overseas inventory, alongside notified interest support and lender guarantees. This is repayable finance, subject to bank approval and current scheme terms.
