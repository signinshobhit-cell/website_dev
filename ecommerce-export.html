BEFORE YOUR FIRST CHANNEL

Questions sellers ask us.

Which marketplace should a new exporter start with?

There is no single best channel for every product. Start with product-policy fit, seller eligibility, total cost, shipping and returns. We can shortlist suitable options and explain what you need before applying.

Can you help with several marketplaces in one plan?

Yes. Select your preferred channels above and send one enquiry. We can coordinate channel selection, onboarding preparation, listings, integrations and export operations, with eligibility and scope reviewed separately for each platform.

Do you provide your own marketplace software?

Our service is setup and coordination. Where a connector is needed, we assess third-party tools for your store and marketplaces, agree the scope and coordinate the setup. Software remains the third-party provider’s product; subscriptions and access are agreed separately.

Can I open an Etsy shop from India?

Etsy’s current India guidance allows new shops to sell internationally and calls for a valid regular GSTIN and Payoneer enrolment. Your products must also meet Etsy’s selling policies.

Are you an official partner of these marketplaces?

Yuvglobal provides independent consultancy. We do not claim an official marketplace partnership, guaranteed approval or guaranteed sales. Marketplace names identify the channels discussed.

How much will the service cost?

Fees depend on the channel, number of products, current account status and work required. Share your requirement for a written scope and quote, separating our fees from platform, software and logistics charges.
