International courier quotes & shipping support

Compare courier estimates for supported export and import routes, then ask our trade desk to confirm the shipment. Yuvglobal’s calculator uses your packed weight, dimensions, destination and ready date. Documents, pallets and shipments needing special handling receive a reviewed quote.

What you can compare online

The live logistics page compares available parcel estimates for supported courier services. Choose export from India or import to India, your country, readiness date and each group of identical pieces. No account or contact information is required to view available estimates. Carrier coverage and weight slabs vary; a missing instant rate leads to manual enquiry.

Why dimensions change a courier estimate

A light but bulky parcel can be billed by volumetric weight. Our estimate compares actual and volumetric weight for each piece, using centimetres and a divisor of 5,000. Dimensions round up to whole centimetres, and estimated billable weight rounds up to half a kilogram per piece. The calculator accepts grams and inches too. Final carrier rules and measurements must be checked before booking.

Documents, pallets and special goods

Use the document or pallet choice to enter measurements and send them for review. Batteries, dangerous, perishable, oversized or heavy goods also require assessment. Describe the goods accurately; an online estimate is not confirmation that a carrier will accept them. For sea freight, choose the manual LCL or FCL enquiry.

What an estimate includes

Displayed transport estimates include the supplier’s fuel and other included surcharges. Taxes, customs duties and optional insurance are excluded. Postcode serviceability, final packed measurements, collection arrangements and applicable rate validity are confirmed before booking. No payment or booking is made by viewing a quote.

Turn an estimate into a shipment enquiry

Open the prepared WhatsApp message or transfer details to the freight enquiry form. Our team checks the route, contents and documentation, and confirms the commercial scope. Do not send identity documents in public quote fields. If you need export setup as well, ask about IEC or the relevant council membership.
