BUILT FOR FIRST-TIME APPLICANTS

Get a clear scope before you apply.

For new importers

Your imported products and their packaging can create different EPR obligations. We review the relevant product lists, business role and quantity records to identify each applicable stream.

For new exporters

Exporting alone does not automatically create an Indian EPR registration requirement. We review domestic supply, manufacturing and packaging activities. Overseas EPR requirements need a separate destination-country assessment.

1. Share your business profile

Business activity, product list, packaging materials, annual quantities and import/export records. Entity documents, GST and IEC are reviewed where applicable.

2. Confirm scope & fees

We identify the relevant rules, exemptions, authority and document checklist. Professional fees and government charges are stated separately in the quote.

3. Apply & stay compliant

Application and query support, followed by agreed assistance with returns, records and obligation tracking. Authority approval determines registration issuance.
