Which benefits belong to your business?

Exporters: assess remission, refunds, inputs, machinery, MSME finance and market support. Importers: duty relief usually depends on authorised use, export obligations or origin; importing alone does not create an export subsidy entitlement. CHA / freight forwarders: assess AEO-LO and eligible project support; clients’ shipment incentives belong to the eligible exporter. Logistics services do not automatically qualify as GST exports.
